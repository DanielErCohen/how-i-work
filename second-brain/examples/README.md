# Examples

Three things to do in the vault once the 10-minute setup is done. Each example shows what you say,
what Claude does and what the vault looks like afterwards. All names, dates and numbers are invented.

1. [Log a workout](01-log-a-workout.md): one sentence becomes one row in a CSV log, with units.
2. [Dictate a story](02-dictate-a-story.md): a voice note is corrected against the registry of names
   and filed as a dated entry.
3. [File the inbox](03-file-the-inbox.md): `/capture` routes loose notes and leaves the ambiguous ones.
