## The failure mode to watch

`03-resources/` is where a second brain goes to die. It has no deadline, no standard, and no natural
pressure to stay small, so everything ambiguous drifts here and stays forever.

Two habits that prevent it:

- Saving something requires a line in `## Why I saved it` (see `note-templates/source.md`). No reason,
  no file.
- Anything here untouched and unreferenced for a year goes to `04-archive/`. It is not lost; it is out
  of the way.
