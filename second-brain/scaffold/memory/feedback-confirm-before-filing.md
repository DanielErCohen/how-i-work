When an item in `00-inbox/` could belong to a project or to an area, ask which one before filing it. Leave it in the inbox until I answer.

**Why:** two notes went into `01-projects/` on a guess, finished projects were archived with them, and the notes were hard to find a month later.

**How to apply:** during `/capture` and `/weekly-review`, list ambiguous items in one table with the two candidate folders. File only the clear ones. Ask one question per ambiguous item, and wait for the answer.
