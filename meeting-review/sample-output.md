## What to change

### 1. You open topics with the answer

In the first month, 6 of the 9 topics you introduced started with a solution.

- 2025-04-09, planning: "I think we need a progress bar on the setup flow, let's scope it."
- 2025-04-23, team sync: "Let's just add a second step to the form and see."
- 2025-04-29, planning: "We should rebuild the settings screen. Can we get an estimate?"

Instead: say who is stuck and where, add one number, and hold your idea until someone else has proposed one. Your own 2025-06-10 opening has the shape: "The problem I want to solve this sprint: 1 in 4 new users never finish setup. Ideas welcome, no solutions yet."

### 2. You defer without a date

12 of 31 decision points were deferred. 7 of the 12 had no date.

Of the 7 undated deferrals, 3 came back in a later meeting and 4 left no trace in these transcripts. Instead: name the day and what gets brought, for example "We decide pricing on 2025-06-05. I bring three options with costs."

### 3. You answer your own questions in team syncs
